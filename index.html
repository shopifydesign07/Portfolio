How It Works

From first message to launch day.

Consult

We talk about your brand, your products, and what's not working on your current site — or what you need built from zero.

Design

I put together a mockup of your homepage and product pages, matched to your brand's colours and audience.

Build

Theme setup, product catalogue, collections, checkout, and payment/shipping rules — built and tested on Shopify.

Launch

Final review together, then we go live — with support after launch if anything needs adjusting.

Why Work With Me

  • International-ready setup. Multi-currency display, USD/GBP pricing, and Shopify Payments or Stripe configured for cross-border checkout.
  • Overlaps your workday. Available evenings PKT for US client calls, and standard daytime hours for UK client calls.
